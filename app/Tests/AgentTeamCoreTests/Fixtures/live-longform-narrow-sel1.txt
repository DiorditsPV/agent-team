     generated.
  2. Canary                                       
     Route a small percentage of customers to the 
     new billing service first, then gradually 
     increase traffic as metrics stay healthy.    
     Problems are caught while the blast radius is
     small. The tradeoff is a longer rollout and 
     the need to run both systems and reconcile 
     data.     
  3. Blue green                            
     Run the new billing service in a full     
     parallel environment and flip all traffic  
     over when it is verified. Rollback is fast   
     because the old environment stays intact. The
     cost is duplicate infrastructure and the     
     risk of data drift or split state during the 
     switch.
  4. Shadow                                 
     Send copies of real production billing       
     traffic to the new service while the old one 
     remains authoritative, then compare outputs. 
     No customer is affected by errors, giving   
     high confidence before cutover. It requires  
     extra effort to diff results and side effects
     must be suppressed.
